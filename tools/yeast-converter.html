Yeast converter

Recipe calls for a type of yeast you don't have? Convert between instant, active dry, and fresh (cake) yeast by weight.

Ratios are commonly cited baking guides, not exact science — brands vary. As a rule of thumb: 1 part instant ≈ 1.33 parts active dry ≈ 2.9 parts fresh yeast, by weight. If substituting active dry for instant, dissolve it in warm water first and allow a bit more rising time.

Good to know

Instant yeast can be mixed directly into dry ingredients. Active dry yeast traditionally needs to be dissolved in warm water (about 105–110°F / 40–43°C) for 5–10 minutes before use, though many modern active dry yeasts work fine mixed in directly too. Fresh (cake) yeast is the most perishable of the three and should be used within a couple of weeks of purchase.

If a dough made with a yeast substitution seems slow to rise, that's normal — give it extra time in a warm spot rather than adding more yeast.
