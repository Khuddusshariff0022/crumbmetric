Yeast converter

Recipe calls for a type of yeast you don't have? Convert between instant, active dry, and fresh (cake) yeast by weight.

Ratios are commonly cited baking guides, not exact science — brands vary. As a rule of thumb: 1 part instant ≈ 1.33 parts active dry ≈ 2.9 parts fresh yeast, by weight. If substituting active dry for instant, dissolve it in warm water first and allow a bit more rising time.
