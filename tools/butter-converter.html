Good to know

US recipes usually measure butter by volume (sticks, cups, tablespoons) because US butter is sold in pre-marked sticks. UK, European, and Australian recipes almost always measure butter by weight in grams, which tends to be more accurate — a "cup of butter" can vary slightly depending on how it's packed or softened, while a gram is always a gram.

If a recipe gives you both a volume and a weight for butter, trust the weight — it's the more reliable number, especially for baking where ratios matter.
