Cup to grams converter

Different ingredients weigh different amounts per cup. Pick an ingredient for an accurate conversion.

Weights are for standard US cups and typical ingredient density (e.g. flour spooned & leveled, brown sugar packed). Real-world weight can vary a little by brand and technique.

Good to know

This is why recipes can go wrong even when you measure "correctly" — a cup of flour can weigh anywhere from about 110g to 150g depending on whether it's sifted, spooned, or scooped straight from the bag and packed down. Weighing ingredients removes that guesswork, which is why most professional bakers and nearly all non-US recipes use grams instead of cups.

For the most consistent results with cup measurements: spoon the ingredient into the cup rather than scooping, then level it off with a knife — don't pack it down (except for brown sugar, which is meant to be packed).
