Good to know

Scaling works perfectly for most ingredients — flour, sugar, liquids, fats — because they respond linearly. A few things don't scale as cleanly: salt and strong spices can taste overpowering if you scale them up too literally, so start with slightly less and adjust to taste. Leavening agents (baking soda, baking powder, yeast) also don't always scale 1:1 past about 2x or 0.5x — large batches sometimes need slightly less leavening, small batches sometimes a touch more.

Bake time and pan size don't scale with the recipe. Doubling a cake recipe usually means using a bigger pan (or two pans), not doubling the time in the oven — check for doneness instead of trusting the clock.
