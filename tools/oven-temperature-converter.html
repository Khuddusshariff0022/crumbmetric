Oven temperature converter

Convert between Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Gas Mark — and see the adjusted setting if your oven is fan (convection) instead of conventional.

Rule of thumb: a fan oven runs about 20°C (≈25°F) hotter than a conventional oven at the same dial setting, so fan ovens are usually set lower. Always check your oven's manual if it differs.
