About CrumbMetric

A small set of baking calculators, built because most "recipe conversion" sites make you scroll past a life story to find a number.

CrumbMetric is a free collection of kitchen conversion tools: scaling a recipe, converting oven temperatures, swapping yeast types, converting butter and ingredient weights, and finding a substitute baking pan. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored.

The goal is simple: get you the right number fast, with enough context to trust it, and get back to baking. No account, no app download, no newsletter pop-up.

The conversions are based on commonly cited baking references and standard US measurements unless noted otherwise. They're a solid everyday guide, not a laboratory standard — if a specific recipe gives you an exact conversion, trust the recipe.

Found a conversion that looks off, or want a tool that doesn't exist yet? Open an issue on the GitHub repository.
